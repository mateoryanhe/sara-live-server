OUR COMMITMENT

Safety, respect, and user control

Sara Live is a global live-streaming and social community for adults. We want people to connect and create without being exposed to exploitation, harassment, fraud, or other serious harm. These standards apply to profiles, live streams, voice rooms, calls, public chat, private messages, short videos, images, comments, and all other user activity on Sara Live.

Respect first Threats, hate, humiliation, coercion, and targeted harassment are prohibited.
Control your experience Use in-app report, block, mute, unfollow, and leave controls where available.
Act on serious harm We may remove content, interrupt streams, restrict features, or terminate accounts.
02

COMMUNITY STANDARDS

What is not allowed

Adults only and authentic accounts

  • Sara Live is intended only for people aged 18 or older.
  • Do not falsify age, impersonate another person, or create or manage an account for a minor.
  • Do not rent, sell, share, transfer, or use accounts to evade an enforcement action.

Sexual content and exploitation

  • Sexual exploitation, trafficking, coercion, solicitation, or non-consensual intimate content.
  • Explicit nudity, sexual acts, pornographic content, or services primarily intended for sexual gratification.
  • Secretly captured intimate content, threats to share intimate material, or sexual harassment.

Violence, hate, and dangerous conduct

  • Credible threats, incitement to violence, glorification of violent extremist or terrorist organizations, or graphic violence intended to shock.
  • Attacks or exclusion based on race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, caste, sex, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, or another protected characteristic.
  • Promotion or sale of illegal drugs, weapons, human trafficking, gambling, or other unlawful goods or services.
  • Content that encourages suicide, self-harm, eating disorders, or dangerous acts likely to cause serious injury.

Harassment, privacy, fraud, and manipulation

  • Bullying, stalking, sexual harassment, repeated unwanted contact, humiliation, or coordinated abuse.
  • Doxxing or sharing another person's private data, including identity documents, addresses, contact details, financial information, or precise location, without authorization.
  • Scams, money laundering, deceptive fundraising, fake giveaways, manipulated transactions, or attempts to move payments off-platform to evade safeguards.
  • Spam, malicious links or code, automated scraping, fake engagement, unauthorized advertising, or disruption of the Service.
  • Copyright, trademark, privacy, publicity, or other rights violations.
Context matters, but safety comes first. Educational, documentary, newsworthy, or counterspeech context does not excuse content that creates an immediate or unlawful risk.
03

CHILD SAFETY STANDARDS

Zero tolerance for CSAE and CSAM

Sara Live prohibits any content or conduct that sexually exploits, abuses, or endangers a person under 18. This prohibition applies even when the content is fictional, computer-generated, animated, altered, or shared as a link.

CSAE Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation: conduct that sexually abuses, exploits, grooms, coerces, traffics, or otherwise endangers a child.
CSAM Child Sexual Abuse Material: any visual depiction of a minor engaged in sexually explicit conduct, including manipulated or computer-generated depictions where prohibited by law.

Strictly prohibited

  • Grooming a child for sexual contact, sexual conversation, or exchange of sexual imagery.
  • Sextortion, sexual blackmail, or threatening a child with real or alleged intimate material.
  • Advertising, soliciting, arranging, or facilitating the sexual trafficking or commercial sexual exploitation of a child.
  • Creating, uploading, requesting, possessing, linking to, selling, or distributing CSAM.
  • Sexualizing minors through images, audio, text, role-play, nudity, fetishization, or sexual commentary.
  • Psychological abuse, coercion, manipulation, or encouraging dangerous activity involving a child.
  • Helping a minor access Sara Live, creating an account for a minor, or bypassing age or safety controls.

Detection and action

We may use automated signals and human review to identify suspected underage use, CSAE, and CSAM. When Sara Live obtains actual knowledge of prohibited material or conduct, we may remove or disable access to it, suspend or permanently terminate accounts, preserve relevant records where lawful, and cooperate with law enforcement. Confirmed CSAM will be reported to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) or the relevant regional authority when required by applicable law.

Do not download, save, screenshot, forward, or redistribute suspected CSAM. Report the account or content location through Sara Live, and contact local law enforcement if a child is in immediate danger.

REPORTING & ENFORCEMENT

How to report a safety concern

  1. 1
    Use the in-app report control

    Open the relevant content, profile, stream, room, call, or conversation and select Report from the available safety menu.

  2. 2
    Choose the closest reason

    Select Child Safety for suspected underage use, grooming, sexual exploitation, or CSAM. Add factual details that help locate and review the concern.

  3. 3
    Protect your experience

    Block, mute, unfollow, or leave where these controls are available. Reporting and blocking are separate actions.

  4. 4
    Escalate immediate danger

    Contact local emergency services or law enforcement if someone faces an immediate threat. In-app reporting is not an emergency service.

What Sara Live may do

Depending on context, severity, and applicable law, Sara Live may remove content, reduce visibility, interrupt a live stream, restrict messaging or creator features, freeze suspicious transactions, issue a warning, suspend an account, or permanently terminate access. We may preserve and disclose relevant information where lawfully required or reasonably necessary to protect people and investigate serious harm.

Where an appeal option is available, use Help & Support in Sara Live. An appeal does not guarantee reversal and does not prevent urgent safety action while review is pending.
