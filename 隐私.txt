Sara Live Global User Registration, Login and Identity Verification Full-Process Compliance Specifications
These Specifications govern the entire process of user registration, login and identity verification for Sara Live global users, and comply with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) of the United States, Indonesia’s PSE regulations, Middle Eastern data-related legislation and regulatory requirements across Southeast Asian jurisdictions. Users shall read and agree to these Specifications in full prior to registering an account.
Qualifications of Registering Entities
1.1 Users registering an account must possess full capacity for civil conduct. The minimum age requirement for accounts that only browse basic platform content is 13 years old. To activate access to livestreaming, top-up payments, private audio calls, short video monetization and creator guild onboarding privileges, users must have reached the age of 18. Users aged 13 to 17 may only view free public content and cannot activate any privileges related to payment, content creation or livestreaming; their use of the account shall be known and approved by their legal guardians.

It is prohibited to impersonate others, submit false information, register accounts using another person’s mobile phone number, or register accounts in bulk through automated programs. The Platform reserves the right to reject fraudulent registration applications, terminate fake accounts and impose permanent bans on relevant devices.
1.3 It is forbidden for one individual to register multiple accounts, engage in account trading, account leasing or account sharing. One device and one mobile phone number may only be bound to one primary account. Bulk registration of marketing or traffic-diversion accounts will directly trigger full-device risk control and permanent account suspension.
Compliance for Login and Identity Verification
2.1 The Platform supports three login methods: mobile phone number login, email login, and authorized login via third-party social accounts (e.g., Google, Facebook). Login via third-party accounts only obtains basic public information including profile pictures and display names; no additional privacy permissions will be mandatorily requested. All permissions are requested via separate pop-up windows and are not tied to access to basic platform services.
2.2 High-risk operations (initiating livestreams, large-value top-ups, withdrawal and settlement, commercial monetization of short videos, account binding modification and account deletion) will trigger mandatory facial identity verification. Facial biometric data shall only be used for identity verification and risk management, shall not be utilized to build commercial user profiles, and will be stored in compliance with applicable local laws and regulations.
2.3 Users shall bear full responsibility for the security of their accounts upon login. Users shall properly safeguard their passwords, verification codes and login devices. The Platform will send risk alerts via SMS and in-app push notifications upon detecting logins from unfamiliar locations or unknown devices. If a user discovers account theft, they shall immediately change their password and contact customer service to freeze the account. All losses arising out of account theft shall be borne solely by the user.
Data Authorization Compliance (GDPR-Aligned)
3.1 Each authorization request during registration will be presented via separate pop-up windows, covering account security notifications, personalized content recommendations, marketing SMS/emails, photo album storage, as well as microphone and camera access permissions. Users may decline any single authorization without losing access to basic browsing functions. Mandatory bundled authorization is prohibited.
3.2 Users located in the European Union and the State of California may withdraw all marketing-related consents at any time and submit requests to delete login activity logs. Following consent withdrawal, the Platform shall cease sending commercial promotional communications to such users.
Risk Control and Account Disposition
4.1 During registration and login, the system will automatically detect emulators, bulk registration scripts, high-frequency registration originating from proxy IPs and other anomalous behaviors. Accounts displaying suspicious characteristics will be subject to registration restrictions or suspended login access.
4.2 Where a user repeatedly violates registration rules (including bulk account registration, account theft, account leasing for traffic diversion, etc.), the Platform reserves the right to permanently suspend the account, clear all virtual assets within the account, and reject any appeals for refunds.
Account Deletion Rules
5.1 Users may submit an active account deletion request via the Privacy Center. A cooling-off period of 7 to 30 days applies (the exact duration varies subject to applicable local laws). Users may revoke their deletion application within the cooling-off period. Upon completion of account deletion, the user’s login information, profile pictures, chat records and short video data will be permanently erased in accordance with applicable laws, save for transaction records and risk control records that the Platform is required to retain by competent regulatory authorities.
5.2 After account deletion, the same identity information cannot be used for re-registration within a specified period, to prevent users from conducting illegal diversion activities by deleting and subsequently re-registering accounts maliciously.

Sara Live Short Video Creator Content Upload Compliance Specifications
These Specifications apply to all short video creators on Sara Live and influencers affiliated with MCN agencies who independently upload short video works. They cover regional content compliance, copyright, commercial promotion, minor protection, cultural and religious standards, and advertising disclosure requirements worldwide. These Specifications carry the same legal effect as the Community Guidelines and User Service Agreement.

1.1 Creators publishing revenue-generating commercial short videos, product sales promotions, and paid embedded content must be at least 18 years of age and complete facial identity verification. Minors may only post daily short videos without commercial revenue and are prohibited from publishing advertisements, product promotion content, or paid collaboration material.
1.2 For short videos uploaded by creators under MCN agencies, the relevant MCN shall bear joint compliance review liability for all uploaded content. In the event of mass violations, the Platform will impose penalties on the MCN’s backend revenue and operational access concurrently.
Baseline Compliance Red Lines for Content Upload (Globally Unified Standards)
2.1 It is strictly prohibited to upload short videos containing pornography, nudity, vulgar suggestive content, violence, gore, drug-related material, gambling, terrorism, hate incitement, racial/regional/religious discrimination, defamation of royal families of any country, politically sensitive information, false rumors, or content inciting offline conflicts.
2.2 Upload of content infringing third-party intellectual property rights is forbidden, including unauthorized film and television clips, music, game footage, trademarks, portraits and audio materials; reposted or plagiarized original short videos of others; as well as AI face-swapping and AI voice-changing content without complete written authorization.
2.3 It is prohibited to upload short videos that induce offline transactions, encourage users to add third-party contact information, divert traffic to external platforms, promote false financial management, advertise unsubstantiated medical aesthetic effects, overstate the therapeutic effects of health supplements, link to gambling, or contain fraudulent scripts.
2.4 Short videos violating others’ right to privacy or right to reputation are prohibited, including malicious photo manipulation for rumor-mongering, doxxing of others’ personal information, cyberbullying and verbal abuse.
Mandatory Disclosure Rules for Commercial Promotion Short Videos (Aligned with U.S. FTC and EU Advertising Legislation)
3.1 All short videos involving brand partnerships, paid placements, commission-based product sales, and sponsorship must carry clear labels such as #ad, Sponsored, or Partnership Promotion prominently at the start of the video, on the cover, or in the title. Subtle acknowledgements or small-font annotations do not satisfy compliance requirements.
3.2 Short videos must not make unsupported claims regarding medical outcomes, weight loss, skin whitening, financial management, or investment returns. Promotions for beauty, health supplement and financial products must be supported by genuine qualification documentation. Non-compliant videos will be removed, and creators’ monetization privileges restricted.
3.3 Content relating to affiliate distribution and rebate promotions must fully disclose the revenue sharing model. Concealing commercial partnerships to mislead users is prohibited.
Regional Localized Compliance Rules for Content Upload
4.1 European Union Region: Without consent, short videos may not capture passers-by’s portraits for user profiling marketing. Data retention shall comply with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Creators may submit requests to delete all data of works uploaded by themselves.
4.2 Indonesia / Malaysia: Short videos must not profane Islamic religious symbols, scriptures and attire, or incite ethnic confrontation. Content data shall be stored locally and shall not be transmitted cross-border without authorization.
4.3 Thailand: It is strictly prohibited to mock, insult, or use edited distorted footage or text related to the royal family. Non-compliant short videos will be removed immediately, and the creator’s account will be subject to severe penalties.
4.4 United States: Marketing short videos shall strictly abide by the Telephone Consumer Protection Act (TCPA) and FTC advertising disclosure rules. False giveaways and inducing users to submit personal information for SMS harassment are forbidden.
Platform Disposition Rules for Uploaded Content
5.1 After creators upload short videos, the Platform implements multi-level content review; violating content will be directly removed. Minor violations will result in traffic restrictions and suspension of upload privileges for 7–30 days. Moderate violations will lead to freezing of short video monetization revenue. Severe violations will result in permanent revocation of creation and upload functions, account suspension and forfeiture of all account assets.
5.2 Compliant short videos will be retained by the Platform for 6–12 months for regulatory verification purposes. Creators may apply to take down their own works. If third-party claims arise from infringing content uploaded by a creator, all compensation liabilities and legal liabilities shall be borne jointly by the uploading creator and their affiliated MCN.
Creator Upload Authorization and Intellectual Property Agreements
6.1 By uploading short videos, the creator grants Sara Live a global non-exclusive royalty-free license permitting the Platform to display, play the works within the platform, and create clipped segments for platform promotion. The creator retains full copyright of the work. The Platform shall not license such short videos to external third parties for commercial use.
6.2 Creators are prohibited from uploading short videos for illegal money laundering, transaction volume boosting, or artificially generating fake traffic. Upon verification, the Platform will freeze all settlement revenue derived from such short videos.
