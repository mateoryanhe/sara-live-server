Data Protection

Sara Live ("we", "our", or "us") provides live streaming, chat, virtual gifts, and related social entertainment services through our mobile application. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use our services.

1. Information We Collect

When you use Sara Live, we may collect the following categories of information:

  • Account information: phone number, password (stored in encrypted form), nickname, avatar, profile details, and user ID.
  • Live streaming content: video, audio, live room titles, chat messages, gifts, and interactions you create or send during broadcasts.
  • Device information: device type, model, operating system version, app version, package name, device identifier, and IP address.
  • Usage information: viewing history, login records, feature usage, crash logs, and performance data.
  • Payment information: purchase records for virtual coins, diamonds, gifts, and premium features. Payment processing may be handled by third-party app stores; we do not store full payment card numbers.
  • Permissions-based data: with your consent, we access camera and microphone for live broadcasting, storage for uploads, and notifications for live alerts.

2. Live Streaming Services

Our core service enables users to watch and host live video broadcasts. To deliver this experience, we process live audio/video streams, room metadata, audience interactions, and moderation signals. Live content may be reviewed by automated systems and human moderators to enforce community guidelines and legal requirements.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide, operate, and improve live streaming and social features.
  • Authenticate accounts, prevent fraud, and protect platform security.
  • Personalize recommendations and display relevant live rooms or content.
  • Process virtual gifts, recharges, and in-app transactions.
  • Send service notifications, security alerts, and customer support responses.
  • Detect violations, enforce policies, and comply with applicable laws.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data only in these situations:

  • With service providers that help us operate live streaming infrastructure, cloud hosting, analytics, content moderation, SMS verification, and customer support.
  • When required by law, regulation, court order, or government request.
  • To protect the rights, safety, and security of users, Sara Live, or the public.
  • In connection with a merger, acquisition, or business transfer, subject to continued protection of your data.

5. Data Retention

We retain personal information only as long as necessary to provide our services, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and meet legal obligations. Live chat logs, transaction records, and account data may be retained for different periods depending on business and compliance needs.

6. Security

We use administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect your data, including encrypted transmission, access controls, and monitoring. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your region, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your personal information, withdraw consent, or request a copy of your data. You can update profile information in the app and contact us to exercise your rights.

8. Children's Privacy

Sara Live is not intended for children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us data, please contact us and we will take appropriate action.

9. International Users

Your information may be processed in countries other than your own. By using Sara Live, you consent to the transfer and processing of your information in accordance with this Privacy Policy and applicable law.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the revised version on this page and update the "Last updated" date. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.
